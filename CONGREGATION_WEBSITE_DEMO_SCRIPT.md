# Mission for Nation Website: Full Demo Recording Script

## Recording Overview

**Audience:** Congregation members, families, and people interested in visiting a Mission for Nation church.

**Estimated length:** 7-10 minutes at a calm speaking pace, depending on how long you pause on each page.

**Purpose:** Show viewers where to find practical church information and how to move around the website. The script is written to be spoken aloud. Text in square brackets is an on-screen direction and should not be read aloud.

**Recommended path:** Home → Services → a church profile → About → Gallery → Home and footer.

**Before recording:** Choose a region and church profile with useful information already visible. Check that images and live content have loaded. Use the English or Amharic site language that suits the audience. Avoid opening external links during the recording.

---

## 1. Welcome and Navigation

[Begin at the top of the Home page. Keep the header visible.]

**Say:**

“Welcome to the Mission for Nation website. This website is here to help our congregation and visitors find church information, learn about the ministry, and stay connected with what is happening across our church community.

“At the top of the page, you will find the main navigation: Home, About, Services, and Gallery. The Mission for Nation logo takes you back to the Home page. You can also switch between English and Amharic using the language button. On a phone, open the menu button to see the same page links.

“I’ll take you through the website now, starting with the Home page, then show you how to find a church location and plan a visit.”

[If appropriate, briefly click the language button to demonstrate the available language switch. Return to the language you will use for the rest of the recording.]

**Optional line if demonstrating the mobile layout:**

“On a smaller screen, the main links are grouped inside this menu, so the same pages are still easy to reach.”

---

## 2. Home Page: The Starting Point

[Show the Home hero at the top. Pause long enough for viewers to see the title and main links.]

**Say:**

“The Home page is the starting point. It introduces Mission for Nation and brings several useful parts of the website together in one place. From here, you can go directly to the church locations, look at program highlights, learn about the ministry, browse photos, and find the main worship schedule and giving information.”

[Point to the Explore Services button without leaving Home yet.]

**Say:**

“If you are looking for a congregation near you, this Explore Services link is a direct route to the church-location directory. I’ll use it in a moment.”

[Scroll to the upcoming events and programs section.]

**Say:**

“Further down the Home page, you may see upcoming events and programs. This section is a quick way to notice current gatherings and special programs. When an event has more information available, its card can show details such as an image, date, or time. The link here can take you to the church’s full program information.”

[Scroll to the About introduction and gallery highlights.]

**Say:**

“The Home page also gives a short introduction to the church and features selected photos from church life. Selecting a gallery highlight takes you to the full Gallery page, where you can browse more posts.”

[Scroll to the worship schedule and giving section.]

**Say:**

“This section shows the worship schedule for the Addis Ababa Jerusalem Main Church. The times are labeled in Ethiopian local time. If you need the schedule for a different congregation, open that location from the Services page and check its own profile.

“Beside the schedule is the giving information. The website lists bank account details for people who wish to support the ministry. The copy button copies the displayed account number; it does not process a payment on the website.”

[If demonstrating Copy, click it only after confirming clipboard access. Do not read out account numbers unless this is intentionally part of the presentation.]

[Continue past the Bible verse and ministry updates/highlights.]

**Say:**

“Below the schedule, Home may also show a Bible verse and ministry updates. The updates can include text, images, or video, depending on what has been published.”

**Transition:**

“Now let’s follow the main visitor journey: finding a church location and checking how to visit.”

---

## 3. Services Page: Find a Church Location

[Select Services in the header, or return to the top and select Explore Services.]

**Say:**

“The Services page is the directory of Mission for Nation church locations. It is designed to help you find a congregation and see which region it belongs to.

“By default, the page shows all available locations. The region list is on the left on a larger screen. Each region can show how many church locations it includes, and a notice count when regional announcements are available.”

[Select a region that has at least one church. Pause for the list and announcements to update.]

**Say:**

“When I select a region, the page narrows the church list to that region and shows announcements associated with it. To return to the full directory, I can choose View All or select All Regions.”

[Point to one church card.]

**Say:**

“Each church card gives a quick preview. Depending on the information available for that location, you may see its name, address or area, a short description, upcoming service times, and the pastor’s name. To get the full information, select View Details on the card.”

[Open the selected church profile.]

---

## 4. Church Profile: Prepare for a Visit

[Start at the top of the profile and show the breadcrumb, church name, and location.]

**Say:**

“This is the profile for the church location we selected. The breadcrumb at the top shows where we are: Home, Services, and then this church. You can use Services in the breadcrumb to return to the directory.

“The profile gathers practical information about this congregation on one page. The quick-navigation links make it easier to go straight to a section instead of scrolling through the whole page.”

[Use the quick-navigation link for Service Times, or scroll to the section.]

**Say:**

“First, check Service Times for the days and times listed for this location. Please note the schedule shown here is for this church profile. If you are planning to attend another location, return to Services and open that church’s profile instead.”

[Show Announcements if the selected profile has them. If it has none, skip this section and do not imply that it does.]

**Say when announcements are visible:**

“Church-specific announcements appear here when they are available. This is a useful place to check for notices that apply to this congregation.”

[Show the leadership and events sections if populated.]

**Say:**

“The profile can also introduce the church’s pastor or leadership and list regular events and programs. These details help visitors get a sense of who serves the congregation and what activities are offered.”

[Show Ministries if populated.]

**Say:**

“Here you can see ministries associated with this church and a short description of each one. If you are interested in getting involved, this is a good place to learn what is available at this location.”

[Use the Location and Contact quick link. Show address, phone/email links, map, and Get Directions.]

**Say:**

“The Location and Contact section helps you plan how to get in touch and how to get there. It may include the address, phone number, email address, and an embedded map. Selecting Get Directions opens a map or directions destination outside the website.

“Phone and email details are links, so on a supported device they can open the phone or email application.”

[If the profile has a gallery, show a few images.]

**Say:**

“Some church profiles also include photos for that congregation or its region. The quick-navigation links remain available in the side panel as you move through the page.”

**Transition:**

“That covers the practical information for visiting a church. Next, I’ll show where to learn about the wider Mission for Nation story.”

---

## 5. About Page: History, Ministry, and Leadership

[Select About from the header. Show the page heading and opening section.]

**Say:**

“The About page is for anyone who wants to understand the church’s background and wider ministry. It introduces the beginnings of Mission for Nation and describes how the church has grown over time.”

[Show the history narrative and photographs.]

**Say:**

“The opening story provides historical context, including the early ministry and its development. Photographs and media are included alongside the story to help bring that history to life.”

[Scroll to achievements or impact. Choose a small number of items to show rather than reading all of them.]

**Say:**

“As you continue down the page, you can explore achievements and areas of ministry impact. These sections describe the work and growth of the church in more detail.”

[Scroll to the leadership or organizational information available on the page.]

**Say:**

“The page also provides leadership and organizational information. This helps visitors understand the people and structure supporting the wider ministry.”

[If showing the featured external video, point it out but do not open it unless leaving the website is part of the recording.]

**Say:**

“There is also a featured video link for viewers who want to explore more. It opens an external site, so I’ll stay on this page for our walkthrough.”

**Transition:**

“Now let’s look at the Gallery, where visitors can explore photos and posts from church life.”

---

## 6. Gallery: Browse Photos and Media

[Select Gallery in the header. Wait for the posts to load.]

**Say:**

“The Gallery brings together photos and media posts from Mission for Nation. At the top, you can choose All Regions or a specific region to narrow the posts shown.”

[Select a region only if the region filter and posts are already loaded.]

**Say:**

“Choosing a region updates the gallery to show posts associated with that area. If you want to return to the full collection, select All Regions.”

[Open one post.]

**Say:**

“Selecting a post opens a larger viewer. You can move to the previous or next item with the arrows. You can also use the left and right arrow keys on your keyboard, and press Escape or select the close button to return to the gallery.”

[Use a next/previous arrow once, then close the viewer.]

**Say:**

“As you scroll, more posts can load automatically, so you can continue browsing the collection. The region selector is the visible filter on this page.”

---

## 7. Footer and Closing

[Scroll to the bottom of Gallery or return to Home and scroll to the footer.]

**Say:**

“At the bottom of the site, the footer repeats the main page links and provides contact and social links. This is another way to return to Home, About, Services, or Gallery without scrolling back to the top.”

[Briefly point out contact/social links without opening them.]

**Say:**

“To recap: start on Home for the main highlights, choose Services to find a congregation, open a church profile to check its schedule and plan a visit, use About to learn the church’s story, and visit Gallery to browse photos and ministry updates.

“Thank you for taking a look at the Mission for Nation website. We hope it helps you find the information you need and connect with a church community.”

[End the recording on Home or on the website page most useful to your audience.]

## Optional 3-Minute Version

Use this shorter narration when the recording needs to be brief.

[Start on Home.]

**Say:**

“Welcome to the Mission for Nation website. The main menu takes you to Home, About, Services, and Gallery, and the language button switches between English and Amharic. Home brings together ministry highlights, worship information, and links to other parts of the site.”

[Select Services.]

**Say:**

“To find a congregation, open Services. You can view all locations or choose a region to narrow the list. Select a church card to open its profile.”

[Open one church profile and show service times and directions.]

**Say:**

“Each church profile can show service times, announcements, events, ministries, leadership, and location details. Use the quick links to jump to a section. The address and directions information can help you plan a visit.”

[Select About, then Gallery.]

**Say:**

“About shares the church’s history and ministry information. Gallery lets you browse photos and media, with a region filter and a larger viewer for each post.”

[Finish on Home.]

**Say:**

“Use Home for the main highlights, Services to find a church and plan a visit, About to learn the church’s story, and Gallery to explore church life. Thank you for visiting the Mission for Nation website.”

## Recording Accuracy Checklist

Before finalizing the recording, confirm the following:

- The selected region and church profile actually appear and have finished loading.
- The selected church’s visible service times and contact information are legible.
- Home worship times are identified as belonging to Addis Ababa Jerusalem Main Church and are labeled Ethiopian local time.
- You describe the giving section as bank account information with a copy button, not as an online payment service.
- You do not promise that visitors can watch a live stream from Home; the live-stream component is not currently displayed there.
- You describe the visible Gallery filtering as region-based. Do not describe category filters as visitor controls.
- You only describe announcements, events, ministries, or photos as present when they are visible for the chosen region or church.
- You do not imply that the site supports visitor accounts, event registration, or content management unless those features are separately confirmed.
- External links are clearly identified before clicking so the audience understands they will leave the website.
