# Mission for Nation Website Demo Guide

## Purpose

This guide is for recording a website demonstration for the Mission for Nation congregation. It explains what visitors can find on each page, how the main navigation works, and how to move through common tasks such as finding a church, checking its service information, and planning a visit.

The central message of the demo is:

> The website helps members and visitors learn about Mission for Nation, find a nearby church, see worship and program information, and connect with the church community.

The guide describes the visitor-facing website as it is currently implemented. It is not a guide to website administration.

## Intended Audience

The demo is for:

- Congregation members looking for service times, announcements, or programs.
- People who are new to the church and want to find a location or plan a visit.
- Families looking for information about a local church and its ministries.
- Visitors who want to learn about the church's history, leadership, and wider ministry.
- People who want to view photos and updates from church life.

## Main Navigation

The website header provides the main route through the site:

- **Home** returns to the opening page and its overview of church information.
- **About** presents the church's background and story.
- **Services** opens the directory of church locations and regions.
- **Gallery** opens photos and media posts.
- The **Mission for Nation logo** returns visitors to Home.
- The **language button** switches between English and Amharic.

On smaller screens, the page links are inside the menu button. The language button remains available beside it. The footer repeats the main page links and includes contact and social links.

A simple navigation principle to explain in the recording is: **start from the top menu, and use Services when you need information about a particular church location.**

## Page-by-Page Guide

### 1. Home

**Purpose:** Give visitors a welcoming overview and provide shortcuts to useful parts of the website.

**What visitors can find:**

- A welcome section introducing Mission for Nation International.
- A prominent route to explore church locations and services.
- Upcoming events and programs when available.
- A brief introduction to the church and its ministry.
- Selected gallery highlights that lead to the full Gallery page.
- Worship times for the Addis Ababa Jerusalem Main Church.
- Giving information, including listed bank account numbers with copy buttons.
- A Bible verse and ministry updates/highlights.
- A link to the external digital library.

**How to explain it:**

> “Home is the starting point. From here, you can get a quick sense of the ministry, see current program highlights, find the worship schedule and giving information, or go straight to the church-location directory.”

**Useful actions to demonstrate:**

1. Point out **Explore Services** and explain that it opens the church directory.
2. Scroll to the upcoming events and note that event details may include a date, time, image, and link to more church programs.
3. Scroll to the worship schedule and giving information.
4. Demonstrate a bank-account copy button only if appropriate for the recording. Confirm the browser permits clipboard access first.
5. Select a photo highlight to open the full Gallery page.

**Important context:** The Home worship schedule is shown in Ethiopian local time and identifies the Addis Ababa Jerusalem Main Church. Visitors looking for another congregation's schedule should open that church's profile from Services.

### 2. About

**Purpose:** Help visitors understand the church's identity, history, leadership, and wider ministry.

**What visitors can find:**

- An introduction to Mission for Nation and its origins.
- The story of the ministry's early beginnings and growth.
- Historical photographs and a featured external video link.
- Highlights describing ministry achievements and impact.
- Leadership and organizational information further down the page.

**How to explain it:**

> “About is where you can learn how Mission for Nation began, how it has grown, and who serves in leadership. It gives new visitors useful context about the church beyond the practical details of a Sunday visit.”

**Suggested demo action:** Show the opening history section, then scroll to one or two achievement or leadership sections. There is no need to read every historical detail aloud; use the page to orient the congregation to where the background information lives.

### 3. Services: Church Locations and Regions

**Purpose:** Help people find a Mission for Nation church and discover information relevant to its region.

**What visitors can find:**

- A directory of church locations.
- A region/network selector with location counts.
- Regional announcements when available.
- Location cards that may include a church name, address/location, short description, service-time previews, and pastor name.
- A link from each location card to its detailed church profile.

**How the region selector works:**

1. The page initially shows all available locations.
2. Selecting a region narrows the list to churches associated with that region.
3. Regional announcements update to reflect the selected region.
4. Selecting the active region again, or choosing **View All**, returns to the full directory.
5. The main Jerusalem/Eyursalem location is prioritized near the top when it appears in the list.

**How to explain it:**

> “If you are looking for a church near you, start here. You can browse all locations or choose a region to narrow the list. The announcements above the church listings help you see regional programs, and each church card opens a more detailed profile.”

**Suggested demo action:** Choose a region that has visible locations and announcements. Show how the list changes, then open a location that has useful detail available.

### 4. Church Profile: A Specific Location

**Purpose:** Put the practical information for one congregation in one place so a visitor can plan to attend or contact the church.

A church profile is reached by selecting a church card on Services. It includes a breadcrumb back to Home and Services, followed by the church's name, location, and description.

**Sections visitors can find:**

- **Service Times:** the location's worship and service schedule.
- **Announcements:** church-specific notices, when provided.
- **Leadership:** pastor or church leadership details.
- **Regular Events:** programs and events at that location.
- **Get Involved / Ministries:** ministry groups and descriptions.
- **Location & Contact:** address, phone, email, map, and directions.
- **Gallery:** photos associated with the church or its region.

**Quick navigation:** The profile's side panel provides jump links to these sections. Visitors do not have to scroll through the entire page to reach service times, contact information, or directions.

**Planning a visit:**

- Visitors can use the **Get Directions** buttons to open a map/directions destination.
- Phone and email links can open the visitor's calling or email app.
- A map is embedded in the profile when location information is available.

**How to explain it:**

> “Once you choose a church, this profile helps you answer the practical questions: when does it meet, what is happening, who can I contact, and how do I get there? The quick-navigation links on the side take you directly to each section.”

**Suggested demo action:** Demonstrate the quick link to service times, then use the directions or location section. If the chosen church has announcements, events, or ministries, show one relevant example.

### 5. Gallery

**Purpose:** Let visitors explore photos and media from church and ministry life.

**What visitors can do:**

- Browse posts in a responsive photo grid.
- Filter posts by region using the visible region controls.
- Select a post to open a larger viewer.
- Move to the previous or next item using on-screen arrows or the keyboard arrow keys.
- Close the viewer with the close button or Escape key.
- Continue browsing additional posts as more items load while scrolling.

**How to explain it:**

> “Gallery is a visual record of ministry and church life. You can narrow the posts by region, open any item for a closer look, and move through the collection without leaving the viewer.”

**Important context:** The visible Gallery filter is region-based. Do not describe category or media-type filters as available controls in the current interface.

## Suggested Recording Flow

This sequence demonstrates the most important congregation-focused journey: finding a church and planning a visit.

### Opening

> “Welcome. This is the Mission for Nation website. It is designed to help members and visitors find church information, explore ministry updates, and connect with a local congregation. I’ll show you where to find those things.”

Start at Home and keep the header visible long enough to introduce the main navigation and the English/Amharic language option.

### Step 1: Introduce Home

> “The Home page brings together the main highlights: church programs, a short introduction to the ministry, gallery highlights, worship times, and giving information. If you already know what you need, the top menu takes you directly to the relevant page.”

Point out the **Explore Services** link. Avoid spending too long on every Home section; the practical journey is the focus.

### Step 2: Find a Church

Select **Services**.

> “The Services page is the church directory. You can see all locations or select a region to narrow the list. Regional announcements appear here too, so you can check for programs in that area.”

Select a region and pause so the changed list is visible. Choose an appropriate church card.

### Step 3: Plan a Visit

On the church profile:

> “This page brings together the details for this congregation: service times, notices, leadership, events, ministries, and location/contact information. The quick links let you jump directly to what you need.”

Use a quick-navigation link to show service times. Then show the address/map/directions controls. If the profile has useful current notices or programs, show one as well.

### Step 4: Explain the Church Background

Select **About** from the header.

> “For anyone who wants to know more about the church, the About page shares its history, growth, achievements, and leadership.”

Show the opening story and one additional section, then return to the header.

### Step 5: Show Church Life

Select **Gallery**.

> “The Gallery lets you browse photos and media from across the ministry. You can filter by region, open an item, and move through the collection.”

Choose a region if useful, open a post, use one navigation control, and close the viewer.

### Closing

Return to Home.

> “To recap: use Services to find a congregation and plan your visit, About to learn the church's story, and Gallery to explore photos and ministry moments. Home brings the main highlights together.”

## Short Alternative Demo: Two Minutes

If recording time is limited, use this short path:

1. **Home:** introduce the purpose and point out **Explore Services**.
2. **Services:** filter by region and open one church.
3. **Church Profile:** show service times and directions.
4. **Gallery:** open one item and demonstrate closing the viewer.
5. End by naming **About** as the place for church history and leadership.

## Rehearsal Checklist

Before recording:

- Open the site at its intended demo URL and confirm the main pages load.
- Confirm the English/Amharic switch works and choose the language for the recording.
- Decide which region and church location to demonstrate.
- Check that the selected location has service times and a usable address or map.
- Confirm there is at least one visible announcement, event, or gallery item to show if your script depends on it.
- Allow live content time to load before recording narration or screen movement.
- Make sure the browser zoom level keeps the page title and relevant controls readable.
- Avoid selecting external links unless you intend to leave the website during the recording.
- Confirm clipboard permission before demonstrating the giving account copy button.
- Keep any personal or sensitive browser information out of the recording.

## Accuracy Notes for the Presenter

- The Home schedule describes the Addis Ababa Jerusalem Main Church; use an individual church profile for a branch-specific schedule.
- Worship times are labeled as Ethiopian local time. Read the displayed time as written, and do not convert it during the demo unless you have verified the conversion.
- The current Home page includes an external digital library link. A separate live-stream component exists in the project, but it is not currently displayed on the Home page. Do not promise that the Home page provides a working live stream.
- The Gallery currently exposes region filtering; do not claim that visitors can filter by post category or media type.
- Some announcements, church details, events, and gallery posts depend on available site data and network access. Describe only what is visible during the recording.
- The website provides visitor-facing church and ministry information. Do not imply that visitors can sign in, manage a profile, register for events, or administer church content unless those capabilities are demonstrated separately.
- Giving information is displayed as bank account details with a copy action. Do not characterize this as an online payment checkout.

## Presenter Vocabulary

Use plain, visitor-focused language:

- Say **“church locations”** or **“congregations”** when describing the directory.
- Say **“choose a region to narrow the list”** rather than implying there is a search box.
- Say **“church profile”** for the detail page opened from a location card.
- Say **“worship schedule”** or **“service times”** for meeting times.
- Say **“open directions”** when demonstrating the map link.
- Describe giving as **“bank account information for supporting the ministry”**, not as a completed online donation.

## One-Sentence Summary

The Mission for Nation website helps congregants and visitors find a church, check its schedule and programs, plan a visit, learn about the ministry, and explore church life through photos and updates.
